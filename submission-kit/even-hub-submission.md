Short description:
Hands-free AI assistant for Even G2. Speak a question or business command, and NIAR returns a concise answer on the lens.

Full description:
NIAR Assistant turns Even G2 into a lightweight front end for a real multi-agent AI operation. The user speaks through the glasses microphone, NIAR processes the request through Futurize IA's assistant backend, and a short, readable answer is rendered directly on the lens.

The current prototype focuses on hands-free question answering and business command previews. It is designed for fast glanceable output, not long chat sessions. Future versions can add CRM lookups, lead alerts, scheduling, translation, and operational commands.

## Value For Even G2

- Shows a practical AI-agent use case on the lens.
- Uses the glasses for short, contextual answers where a phone screen is unnecessary.
- Demonstrates voice-in and lens-out interaction for business productivity.
- Keeps the interface minimal and glanceable for daily use.

## Permissions Explanation

Microphone:
Used only when the user asks a question or gives a voice command through the glasses.

Network:
Used to send the user's question to the NIAR assistant backend and return a concise response to the lens.

## Privacy Summary

NIAR Assistant processes voice prompts to generate answers. Audio or transcripts are sent to Futurize IA's backend only for the purpose of responding to the user's request. The app does not sell user data and does not use prompts for advertising targeting.
